AboutBraxMOV

I’m Braxton Joy, a Brand World Architect and founder of BraxMov.

I work with independent product brands that have something worth selling but refuse to disappear into a crowded market.

My background is rooted in storytelling, filmmaking and creative strategy. As a published author and narrative director, I’ve spent years studying how visuals, language, emotion and story shape the way people experience an idea.

That’s what I bring to brands. I take the product, the vision and what already makes the business distinct, then architect the creative world around it.

Marketing brings people to the door.

I help determine what world they walk into.
